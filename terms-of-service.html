02Description of Service

Budget Flow is a personal finance tracking application that allows you to:

  • Record income and expenses, including by voice
  • Organize transactions into categories and separate accounts
  • Set up recurring transactions and reminders

  • See your monthly totals in a home screen widget

Budget Flow is a personal record-keeping tool. It is not intended to provide financial, investment, or tax advice, and it does not connect to your bank or move money.
