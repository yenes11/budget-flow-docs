01Introduction

Budget Flow ("the App") is developed and published by Enes Yurtsever, an independent developer based in Türkiye ("we," "our," or "us"), who is the data controller for the purposes of this Privacy Policy. This Privacy Policy explains what information is processed when you use the App, why, and what choices you have.

Budget Flow has no accounts, no ads, and no analytics or tracking. The financial records you enter are stored only on your device. We do not receive or have access to them.

02Data Stored on Your Device

Everything you enter into the App, such as transactions, amounts, notes, categories, accounts, recurring transactions, and your settings, is stored locally on your device. This data:

  • Is not sent to us or to any server operated by us or on our behalf
  • Is not accessible to us or to RevenueCat
  • May be included in your device backups (for example, iCloud Backup), depending on your device settings. Such backups are handled by Apple or Google under their own privacy policies
  • Leaves the App only when you choose to share it, for example when you export your data as a CSV file and share it using your device's share sheet

03Information Processed by Third Parties

To sell and manage subscriptions, the App uses the following services:

  • Apple App Store / Google Play: processes your payments. We never receive your name, email address, or payment details. See Apple's and Google's privacy policies.
  • RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc., USA): manages subscription status. When you open the App or make a purchase, RevenueCat receives a random anonymous identifier generated by the App, your purchase and subscription history (products, prices, trial and renewal status, transaction identifiers), and technical information such as device model, operating system version, app version, country or store region, language, and IP address. This information is used only to verify your purchases, restore them, and provide subscription access. It is not linked to your identity and is not used for advertising or tracking. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

We may see aggregated or pseudonymous subscription information (for example, the number of trials and renewals) in the RevenueCat dashboard. We do not sell your information or share it for advertising.

04Voice Input

If you choose to add a transaction by voice, the App asks for microphone and speech recognition permissions. Your speech is converted into text by Apple's speech recognition service. Depending on your device and language, this may happen on the device or on Apple's servers, and Apple may receive the audio, the selected language, and the names of your categories (used as hints to improve recognition). This processing is subject to Apple's privacy policy.

We do not receive or store your audio recordings. The recognized text is used only to fill in the transaction you are creating. Voice input is optional, and you can revoke these permissions at any time in your device settings.

05Notifications and Widget

  • Notifications: if you allow them, the App schedules reminders (such as a daily reminder to log expenses or a reminder before your free trial ends) locally on your device. No push notification server is used.
  • Widget: the home screen widget shows your monthly totals using data shared between the App and the widget on your device only.

06Legal Basis and International Transfers

Where data protection laws such as the GDPR or the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law (KVKK) apply, we process the subscription information described above because it is necessary to perform our contract with you (providing the subscription you purchased) and for our legitimate interest in preventing fraud and keeping the App working.

RevenueCat processes this information in the United States. Such transfers rely on the safeguards offered by the service provider, such as standard contractual clauses, as permitted by applicable law.

RevenueCat keeps subscription records for as long as needed to provide the service and to meet legal and accounting obligations. Data stored on your device is kept until you delete it.

07Your Rights and Choices

  • Access and portability: all your records are available in the App, and you can export them as a CSV file at any time.
  • Deletion: you can delete your records in the App, or delete everything by uninstalling the App. Uninstalling does not cancel your subscription; manage it in your App Store or Google Play account settings.
  • Subscription data: depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or object to the processing of the subscription information held by RevenueCat, and to lodge a complaint with your data protection authority. Because this information is not linked to your identity, please contact us and include your purchase details so we can help.

08Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us.

09Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.
